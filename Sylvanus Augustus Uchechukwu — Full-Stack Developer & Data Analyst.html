What to expect when we work together. Anything else, ask me directly.

Ask a question

Two things, alone or together: building web applications from database to interface, and analysing and visualising your data. Most projects touch both.

Yes. I start with a short review of the code and data, we agree what to fix or build first, and I work in your repository following your team's conventions.

For development: React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Node, Express, NestJS, EJS and FastAPI, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB. For data: SQL, Python, pandas, Power BI and Excel. I choose what fits your stack rather than the other way round.

A focused data analysis or dashboard usually takes one to three weeks. A full web application takes six to twelve depending on scope. You get a fixed timeline up front and progress updates every week.

Carefully. I’m happy to sign an NDA before we start, work only with the access I need, keep client data off personal devices and delete it when the engagement ends.
